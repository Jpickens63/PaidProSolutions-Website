One person, no dependencies. The decision matrix chooses the next available task using working days to deadline, priority and remaining hours per day. That task runs to completion before another starts; a running Time Card task stays first. Scheduled Date in Work sets the earliest start. Extra hours are added within your daily limit. Past deadlines are flagged, never backdated. Extra-hours estimates apply to this matrix plan; they are not a global optimum or overtime authorization.

Decision matrix

When a task finishes, available tasks receive a score. The highest score goes next. Working days left is counted through the due date, with a minimum of one.

FactorContribution to score
Deadline proximity1,000,000 ÷ working days left
PriorityUrgent 2,000 · High 1,000 · Normal 500 · Low 0
Remaining work(Remaining hours ÷ working days left) × 10

A running task keeps its place until its remaining estimate is finished. Scores choose the next task; they do not divide daily hours between tasks.
